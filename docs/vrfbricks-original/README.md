# Original vrfbricks.github.io

`index.html` is the original site, kept verbatim as the provenance record for
the rebuild at `frontend/src/app/vrfbricks/`. Every fact in
`frontend/src/data/vrfbricks/business.ts` (address, hours, phone, brick sizes,
truck loads, founding year) was carried over from this file.

## Why the images are not here

The original had 19 images in `foo/`. Exactly one of them, `b12L.jpg`, was a
genuine photograph of the yard at Kavali, and it was never used on the site.
It now lives at `frontend/public/vrfbricks/photos/yard-kavali.jpg` and is the
hero of the new home page.

The other 18 were manufacturer catalogue shots, stock photography and one piece
of clip art, including a photo of hollow blocks, which is a product this yard
does not make. None of them depicted this business, none are used in the
rebuild, and keeping 3.4 MB of another company's marketing images in the repo
served no purpose. They were removed.

See `docs/VRFBRICKS.md` for the photography rules the rebuild follows.
